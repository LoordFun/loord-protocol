# Loord Whitepaper

## Vision

Liquidity should not be a fixed shape. A pool is a small market with its own audience, its own risks and its own goals, yet most pools on most chains share one template and one set of rules. Loord imagines pools that carry their own behaviour: a gate that opens when the creator says so, a fee that rises and falls with the mood of the market, a price record other programs can lean on, a cut for the people who made the coin.

## Mission

Give every pool on Solana a socket for small programs, and make writing, sharing and reusing those programs as easy as opening the pool itself.

## How it works

1. A pool is opened with a pair and a price band.
2. A socket program is attached at creation.
3. Every trade and deposit passes the program before and after the pricing curve runs.
4. The program can allow, reprice or refuse, and can keep its own records.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1 · Foundations.** Core pool maths and the socket interface take shape in the open.

**Phase 2 · First sockets.** A starter shelf of programs for gating, fees, price memory and maker cuts.

**Phase 3 · Open floor.** Public pools, a shelf for community programs, and tooling for builders who want to wire their own.

**Phase 4 · Wider reach.** Routing partners, integrations and sockets written by the community.
